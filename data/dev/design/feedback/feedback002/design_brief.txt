Design a single-ended transimpedance amplifier (TIA) with a feedback capacitor (integrator-style).

Assumptions for verification (idealized op-amp):
- Single-pole op-amp with parameters: Aol = 100k V/V, GBW = 10 MHz
- AC verification uses a 1 A AC current injected into the summing node; transimpedance is measured as |Vout| (Ohms)

Target specs to meet (measured by ngspice):
- Transimpedance magnitude at 10 kHz: 159 kΩ ±15%  (this corresponds to Cf ≈ 100 pF via |Z| = 1/(2π f C))
- -3 dB bandwidth (relative to 10 kHz magnitude): ≥ 2 MHz

Provide a SPICE-like netlist that instantiates an `opamp` subcircuit and connects the feedback capacitor correctly.
