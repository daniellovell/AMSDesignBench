Design an inverting voltage amplifier with a resistive feedback network.

Assumptions for verification (idealized op-amp):
- Single-pole op-amp with parameters: Aol = 100k V/V, GBW = 10 MHz
- AC verification uses a 1 V AC input; gain is measured as |Vout/Vin| (V/V)

Target specs to meet (measured by ngspice):
- Closed-loop gain magnitude at 10 Hz: 10 V/V ±5%
- -3 dB bandwidth (relative to 10 Hz gain): ≥ 1 MHz

Provide a SPICE-like netlist that instantiates an `opamp` subcircuit and connects the input resistor and feedback resistor correctly.
