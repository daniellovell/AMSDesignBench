Design a single-ended transimpedance amplifier (TIA) with a feedback resistor.

Assumptions for verification (idealized op-amp):
- Single-pole op-amp with parameters: Aol = 100k V/V, GBW = 10 MHz
- AC verification uses a 1 A AC current injected into the summing node; transimpedance is measured as |Vout| (Ohms)

Target specs to meet (measured by ngspice):
- Transimpedance magnitude at 10 Hz: 10 kΩ ±10%
- -3 dB bandwidth (relative to 10 Hz magnitude): ≥ 5 MHz

Provide a SPICE-like netlist that instantiates an `opamp` subcircuit and connects the feedback resistor correctly.
